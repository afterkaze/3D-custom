    Change an image

    Pick another online version, or use a PNG, JPG or WebP of your own. Only the chosen type is replaced.

    Real cartridge photos and famous coloured cartridges are used automatically.
    Matches PDF manuals to your games by name, for the whole library.

    Sources

    1. LaunchBox Games DatabaseFull-size box scans, discs, cartridges and screenshots. No account needed.
    2. ScreenScraperOptional. Matches by file, good for rare and regional releases.
    3. GameTDB & LibretroFallbacks: Switch labels, and smaller covers for everything else.
    ScreenScraper login

    Use your free screenscraper.fr account. ScreenScraper also requires a developer ID, which developers request on the ScreenScraper forum.

    Settings

    Set a ROM folder for each system. To use several folders (e.g. two SD cards), click “+ Add” or separate paths with |.

    Prompt labels follow your physical controller, regardless of the emulated system. Override the family if your adapter reports a generic name.

    O toggles the overlay on or off. I independently shows or hides the game details.

        Artwork folders

        For each game PyroCade looks in <Artwork folder>\<system>\, then <ROM folder>\media\, then the ROM folder itself. Either layout works:

        One folder per game                 ES-DE / Skraper style
ps2\Neon Drift (USA)\front.png      ps2\covers\Neon Drift (USA).png
                    \back.png          \backcovers\…
                    \spine.png         \physicalmedia\…   (disc / cart)
                    \disc.png          \manuals\….pdf
                    \manual.pdf        \screenshots\…
                    \screenshot.png    \fanart\…
                    \fanart.jpg        \videos\….mp4
                    \video.mp4
                    \info.json  {"title","year","developer","publisher","genre","players","description"}

        An EmulationStation gamelist.xml in the ROM folder is also read for titles and descriptions. Anything missing gets generated art.
